Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Do I have to buy my own hosting and domain?
We recommend and can arrange premium hosting tailored to your site's needs. For the Bronze plan, you can use your own hosting. Our Silver and Gold plans include optimized hosting setup with CDN and daily backups.
Can I easily edit my own text and images later?
Absolutely. We build your site on a custom, user-friendly WordPress dashboard with an intuitive block editor — so you can update text, images, and even add new pages without touching a line of code. We also provide a quick walkthrough video after launch.
How do revisions work?
Each package includes a set number of revision rounds (1 for Bronze, 3 for Silver, 5 for Gold). You submit feedback via our simple request form, and we implement changes within 2–3 business days. Additional revision rounds can be purchased separately.
Is there ongoing support after launch?
Yes! All plans include a maintenance period (1–3 months depending on the plan). After that, you can subscribe to our affordable monthly maintenance packages which include security updates, weekly backups, uptime monitoring, and priority email support.
What if I need M-Pesa integration on my site?
It's one of our specialities. We integrate M-Pesa C2B, Lipa Na M-Pesa, and STK Push directly into your WooCommerce store or custom web application. We also support integrations with major Kenyan banks for a seamless local payment experience.
How fast will my WordPress site load?
Every Cyzora WordPress site scores 95+ on Google Lighthouse with sub-second load times. We use lightweight themes, optimized databases, image compression, CDN integration, and advanced caching to ensure your site is among the fastest in your niche.
